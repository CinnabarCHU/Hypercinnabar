# Interstitial-water chemistry in the Initial Reports

Three numerical profile figures per site, covering Sites 844–854. The 33 vector PDFs contain 116 panels and 1,573 numeric symbols from the 149 shipboard pore-water records. Reading the original chapter alongside the chemical data exposed differences in depth, hole assignments, printed units and individual profiles.

* [manifest.csv](manifest.csv) identifies each figure, chapter PDF and page, plot PDF, plot extent, table, raw-file paths and SHA-256 digests. The source PDF hash identifies the witness used for geometry and wording.
* [plot_layout.json](plot_layout.json) records the panel frames, lettering and caption baselines in a 950 × 1240 reference grid for each physical PDF page. It also records concentration and depth scales. The figures use monochrome vector paths and symbols, Helvetica for axes and Times for the captions. Historical font files were unavailable. Individual tick sizes and line weights are approximate.
* `points.csv.gz` is the plotting ledger. A row links its panel to the exact CHEM line, published value, sample, CD depth and displayed depth. Printed symbol keys and electronic hole assignments can be compared directly. Seven ammonia readings below detection and five entries marked as missing remain in the ledger, without invented numeric ordinates. Connecting segments join available numeric samples in depth order. The catalogue describes CHEM as a reported laboratory product. IR p.297 specifies charge balance as the derivation for the Site 846 sodium profile.
* `geometry_checks.csv.gz` gives the transformed coordinates and the distance to the nearest dark scan pixel for every plotted symbol. Among 1,421 interior symbols, 1,404 land within five reference-grid pixels of ink. The other 17 were inspected. The 152 symbols within eight pixels of a frame boundary are flagged separately because axis ink contaminates this test. Proximity to ink can also reflect a connecting line and does not establish numerical equivalence on its own.
* [geometry_summary.csv](geometry_summary.csv) gives the figures separately. Figures with a known table/graphic discrepancy are called out in the manifest.

At Site 844, twelve depths lose their leading digit(s) in the CHEM version. Printed Table 13 and its second ASCII distribution agree. The redraw uses their depths and retains the CHEM literals. Figure 30 itself prints “884B”. At Site 846, Table 9's heading and Figures 25–27 give 846C while the table body and CHEM identify the 15 deeper samples as 846B. At Site 848, six published B-hole symbols correspond to A-hole electronic sample labels. At Site 852, the main table and continuation group five electronic A-hole samples under B. [hole_variants852.csv](hole_variants852.csv) locates them on printed p.997.

At Site 849, the printed chloride curve in Figure 24B sits about 3–4 mM to the right of the values distributed in Table 9. At Site 852, the final two-panel Figure 27 shows its deepest plotted symbols slightly shallower than the 119.0 mbsf row of Table 12. At Site 853, the top open silica marker in Figure 23D lies near 525 µM whereas the published row gives 451 µM. All three redraws retain the numeric witness. Site 850 Figure 29 labels sodium in µM while Table 11 labels it in mM. The chart preserves the original numeric scale and printed unit. At Site 851, the left endpoint glyph on the Sr and ammonia tracks looks like an “8” even though the subsequent ticks calibrate the coordinate origin to zero. Figure 31 keeps the visible glyph.

From the Leg138 directory, regenerate the PDFs, plotting ledger and manifest with Python 3 and ReportLab.

```sh
python revision/figures_ir/render.py
```

With the original PDFs and CHEM/TABLES files downloaded beneath a single root, verify the byte identities and independently repeat the position checks with PyMuPDF, NumPy, Pillow and SciPy.

```sh
python revision/figures_ir/compare.py --sources /path/to/Leg138
```

The per-sample compressed ledgers are generated by the scripts above. They are also included in the offline edition under `revision_evidence/`. The public figure files, source identifiers and numerical summaries can be inspected independently.
