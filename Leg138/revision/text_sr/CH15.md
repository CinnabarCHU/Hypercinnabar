## Reading and reconstruction decisions

| Question | Printed evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Which depth? | p. 339 distinguishes pipe-based mbsf, between-hole mcd and revised within-core rmcd. The authors say that depth in the remainder of the chapter implies rmcd unless stated otherwise. See `SR.C15.p003.b006`. |
| Which measurements? | p. 339 says that adjustments were made for taxon-dependent departures using Table 4, that all individual oxygen measurements were plotted, including overlapping holes, and that Figure 2 shows the “raw δ¹³C data.” The checked text retains “raw.” See `SR.C15.p003.b007`. |
| Which processing? | p. 341, Figure 1 gives Gaussian interpolation at intervals of 10 cm with a 59.99 cm window. p. 342, Figure 2 refers back to the Figure 1 line. p. 346, Figure 5 gives 3-k.y. spacing and 8.99-k.y. width for the age plot. p. 348 gives 3-k.y. spacing and 5.99-k.y. width for the time-series analyses and identifies the gap code as -9999.00. |
| Where do offsets enter? | p. 341, Table 4 gives the offsets for raw Table 3 data before merging. p. 343, the Table 5 note explicitly averages the adjusted δ¹⁸O and δ¹³C measurements for each sample. The modern Figure 2 reconstruction's adjusted-carbon column and its checks against the plot have their independent reconstruction evidence. |
| Carbon or oxygen in Figure 9? | p. 351, Figure 9 caption prints **δ¹³O**, even though p. 348 describes Figure 9 as δ¹³C and Figure 6 as the carbon isotope record. The original O is preserved in `SR.C15.p015.b002`. |
| Which unit is printed? | p. 338 prints −2.20%, +1.95%, and ±0.06% in the standards and reproducibility discussion. The body on pp. 349–352 likewise prints a percent sign in isotopic differences. The printed Table 7 column labels on p. 354 show the per-mille symbol. The body text keeps the glyph visible in each passage. |
| Which ambiguities survive collation? | The Table 3 note on p. 340 prints “Seris 9” and “calandar year.” On p. 348 the alternatives jump from (4) to (6). On p. 353 the Shackleton and Pisias reference prints CO² in its title, while other references on the same leaf print CO₂. Each reading reflects the specified digital witness. |
