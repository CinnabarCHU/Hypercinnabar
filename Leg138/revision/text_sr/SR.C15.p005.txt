OXYGEN ISOTOPE STAGES

Emiliani (1955) first devised the numerical scheme that is now
almost universally used to designate successively older glacial and
interglacial episodes indicated in the marine δ¹⁸O record. This scheme
was firmly codified by Shackleton and Opdyke (1973), when they
showed that the record for the last several million years can reliably
be taken as a global picture that is dominated by the effect of major
fluctuations in the volume of ice on the northern continents.

Raymo et al. (1989) and Shackleton et al. (1990) erected a numeri-
cal scheme that extended to stage 104 at about the Gauss/Matuyama
boundary and that had demonstrable utility in the sense that every one
of these stages can be recognized clearly in both DSDP Site 607 in the
