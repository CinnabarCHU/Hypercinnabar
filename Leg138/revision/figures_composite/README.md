# Site 846, IR Figure 7

[The three-page digital figure](IR11.F07.pdf) preserves the original arrangement and replaces the GRAPE and susceptibility track interiors with vectors calculated from published numeric data. [The comparison](IR11_F07_Comparison.pdf) pairs all three continuations with their original printed pages. The source is the Site 846 Initial Reports chapter, PDF pp. 12–14, printed pp. 276–278. The comparison uses the IR scan and its own tick coordinates.

## What the data support

The left stratigraphic columns preserve the authors' published magnetics, age, lithologic drawing and unit assignments. The color track retains its optical series and legend from the IR scan. The surviving per-hole optical files contain three measured bands, but the overlapping monochrome curves on this IR figure do not furnish an independent value-by-value check for each band. The earlier shipboard PR redraw is a separate edition.

[regions.json](regions.json) records the page-specific crop, upper and lower panel edges in PDF points, tick labels and scale change at 300 mcd. GRAPE spans 1.3–2.0 g/cm³ throughout. The susceptibility frame spans −1 to 6 in the upper two continuations and −10 to 60 below 300 mcd, in printed units of 10⁻⁵ SI. No amplitude shift or depth shift is fitted to improve agreement. The retained background is a lossless crop from each original decoded JPEG. Only the two re-rendered track interiors are masked. Tick strokes are regenerated and the printed note on the scale change is retained.

`series.csv.gz` gives all 17,354 selected GRAPE rows and 3,727 susceptibility rows. Of these, 17,313 and 3,719 respectively lie in the 0–450 mcd figure window. Each row identifies the distributed file, line number, splice interval, hole, core, section, composite depth, released value and displayed value. The release includes the separately published smoothed GRAPE column. The independent reconstruction applies a 20-sample Gaussian to the measured GRAPE column and to the FIN susceptibility. For susceptibility the per-hole FIN rows are selected with the published SPLICE switch file and multiplied by 0.63, following the explanatory notes' SI conversion. The IR caption specifies a 20-point Gaussian filter. Its weights, precise inputs and splice decisions have not been recovered. The display kernel has σ = 4 samples and renormalizes at the ends. Curves break at a hole, source, core or section change, reversal of composite depth or a sample gap over 0.30 m.

The six underlying physical data and splice files are listed with their URLs and hashes in [source_tracks.csv](source_tracks.csv). [layers.csv](layers.csv) distinguishes the two independently plotted channels from the six retained publication layers on each continuation.

## Comparison with printed IR profiles

At regularly spaced composite depths, the program compares the predicted value with the median position of the dark scan pixels in the same horizontal band. It excludes the frame edges, ambiguous overlapping source runs, near-blank rows and dense ink bands. [scan_comparison.csv](scan_comparison.csv) gives the sample counts, rank correlations and absolute differences. Finite line thickness, slight scan skew and the unidentified production filter limit interpretation.

The density profile follows the main downcore shape but remains systematically displaced. On the three pages, median absolute deviations are approximately 0.13, 0.13 and 0.15 g/cm³. Applying the distributed GRAPECOR correction moves the predictions farther away from the printed values on all three pages. The susceptibility discrepancy is smaller. The unconverted alternative fits the amplitude better on the lower page, where the published axis changes, while the documented SI conversion fits better above it. These tests preserve candidate explanations without assigning an undocumented historical production stage.

## Rebuild

Run from Leg138 with NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, PyMuPDF, Pillow and ReportLab. SOURCE_ROOT needs the IR chapter at ir/primary/VOLUME/CHAPTERS/ir138_11.pdf and the six files under disc/primary identified in source_tracks.csv. Each source SHA-256 is checked. The figures, comparison, row export and evidence tables regenerate together.

```sh
python revision/figures_composite/render.py SOURCE_ROOT --out /tmp/site846-ir-figure7
```

The per-sample compressed ledgers are generated by the scripts above. They are also included in the offline edition under `revision_evidence/`. The public figure files, source identifiers and numerical summaries can be inspected independently.
