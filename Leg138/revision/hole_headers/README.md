# Site-chapter hole summaries

The 11 shipboard site chapters open with the operations coordinates, dates, depths and recovery statistics for 42 holes. All 546 addressed field lines (13 per hole) were visually checked against the source scans. The separate start/end dates, elapsed time, rig-floor and sea-level depths, penetration, number of cores, cored length, recovered length and recovery percentage retain the original decimal precision, punctuation and historically inconsistent readings. Coordinates use a Unicode prime and compact spacing for degrees and minutes.

[Field readings and publisher OCR](readings.csv) preserves both layers, their OCR line addresses and physical page boxes. Ten position lines contain substantive OCR errors, including hemisphere and digit substitutions. All printed recovery percentages above 100 remain as published. In Hole 852B the IR prints 4985.0 m total depth, 3871.6 m bottom felt and 113.4 m penetration. The resulting 1000.0 m residual is retained in the [arithmetic checks](../../data/ir_depth_checks.csv). Hole 851D prints 3759.3 m water depth. The textual source includes the historical spelling “recoved” in ten labels and the singular “Number of core” in Hole 847D. These remain in the reading layer.

PDF boxes use top-left origin in points. Block and line numbers are from the publisher's embedded text layer. Raw source strings in the previous catalogue remain addressable. The modern readers and the local API expose the checked readings alongside those strings. The public registry does not infer a change of survey datum or redistribute nearby holes to different coordinates.
